# Lucent Strategies Group engagement runbook

## Commercial boundary

Lucent bought a four-to-six-week advisory engagement, not an outsourced build. The fixed fee is $7,500 for approximately 20 hours. Britt diagnoses, recommends, reviews, and course-corrects. Lucent owns hands-on implementation. Website work, campaign execution, CRM administration, ongoing sales or delivery staffing, and operation of the finished systems are outside scope unless added by change order.

The engagement succeeds when Lucent can make and implement five decisions:

1. What is the binding constraint on growth?
2. Which buyer and problem should Lucent organize around first?
3. Which three to five offers form a coherent ladder, with scope and price?
4. How will relationships become qualified conversations predictably?
5. What minimum intake-to-delivery process protects quality as volume grows?

## Use of DRIVE

DRIVE is Britt's internal diagnostic and synthesis engine. It is not the client experience or a substitute for judgment.

Use:

- Discover to capture the authoritative operating profile, buyers, offers, constraints, proof, revenue motion, and baseline.
- Intuit to extract and rank signals from the website, proposals, case studies, sales conversations, and Traction materials.
- Visualize to test positioning, offer architecture, referral mechanics, intake, practice systems, and the 90-day action plan.
- Portfolio or Recommendations to rank the few interventions that matter and record why.

Use only if evidence requires it:

- Resolve for definitions that are genuinely contested, such as qualified lead, diagnostic, implementation, or ongoing support.
- Execute only to define reusable content or sales-enablement templates already promised by the operating manual. Do not create a campaign or content calendar.

Do not expose raw AI output. Every client-facing conclusion must be edited, evidence-linked, and presented as a decision or recommendation.

## Week 1: August 24 to August 28

### Before kickoff: maximum 90 minutes

1. Send the bounded intake request in `lucent-kickoff-intake.md`.
2. Create the Lucent engagement record in DRIVE only after receiving permission to use the supplied materials there.
3. Load the public site, current service/pricing page, AI/data playbook, Traction output, up to three representative proposals, up to three case studies or completed-engagement examples, and any existing intake or delivery templates.
4. Enter known facts as provisional. Label public evidence separately from client-supplied evidence.
5. Do not pre-solve positioning or rebuild their materials before the founders identify the operating constraint.

### Kickoff: 90 minutes

**0–10 minutes: contract the session**

- Confirm the business outcome: grow beyond founder-dependent selling and delivery without degrading quality.
- Confirm that Britt will design and advise, while Lucent will implement.
- Confirm one decision owner. Pearson is the presumed owner unless the team says otherwise.
- Confirm that disagreements among founders will be surfaced rather than averaged away.

**10–30 minutes: revenue and demand reality**

- What has been sold in the last 12 months, to whom, for how much, and through which relationship?
- Which work was profitable, repeatable, and energizing?
- Which offers exist on paper but have not been bought?
- Where does a real prospect stall: awareness, qualification, proposal, price, proof, or timing?
- What must be true by December 31 for this engagement to have mattered?

**30–50 minutes: buyer, problem, and proof**

- Who has enough urgency and authority to buy now?
- What event makes the problem non-optional?
- What result can Lucent prove, rather than merely describe?
- Which work requires Pearson, Harrison, or Ian specifically?
- Where does the current “scale enablement” story create clarity, and where does it require explanation?

**50–70 minutes: delivery constraint**

- Walk one recent opportunity from introduction through handoff.
- Identify where scope, pricing, ownership, data access, or client responsibility becomes ambiguous.
- Identify repeatable delivery stages and founder-specific judgment.
- Determine current capacity and the first failure point if demand doubled.

**70–82 minutes: referral motion**

- List the ten relationships most capable of creating a qualified introduction.
- Identify when and how Lucent currently asks.
- Define a qualified introduction and a disqualifying condition.
- Determine what proof or language a referrer needs to make the introduction confidently.

**82–90 minutes: decisions and owners**

- Read back the provisional binding constraint.
- Assign missing evidence and owners, due within two business days.
- Confirm the midpoint decision meeting.
- State what Britt will return with: constraint map, decision questions, and the proposed diagnostic path, not finished deliverables.

### After kickoff: maximum 2.5 hours

1. Complete the DRIVE Discover record and a first-pass Intuit extraction.
2. Produce a one-page current-state constraint map with five sections: demand, offer, proof, conversion, delivery.
3. Score each section 1–5 using evidence, not founder confidence.
4. Name one binding constraint and no more than two contributing constraints.
5. Record unresolved founder disagreements as decisions required.
6. Send a concise readback containing:
   - what appears true;
   - what remains unproven;
   - the provisional binding constraint;
   - the evidence still needed;
   - the decisions scheduled for week 2.

### Week 1 definition of done

- One authoritative fact base exists.
- The team agrees on, or explicitly disputes, the binding constraint.
- Missing evidence has an owner and due date.
- No positioning, website copy, offer names, referral outreach, or systems implementation has been completed prematurely.
- Britt has used no more than 5.5 hours in total.

## Diagnostic rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. A score is only valid when supported by observed evidence.

| Dimension | Core question | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Priority buyer | Is one buyer specific enough to recognize themselves and buy? | Won work, pipeline, buyer interviews, proposal recipients |
| Urgent problem | Is the problem expensive or risky enough to act on now? | Trigger events, objections, timing, budget behavior |
| Offer coherence | Can buyers understand the entry, implementation, and continuation path? | Service page, proposals, price, scope, conversion |
| Differentiated proof | Can Lucent prove why its approach is safer or more valuable than DIY tools or generalists? | Case evidence, artifacts, references, outcomes |
| Qualification | Does Lucent protect founder time and reject weak-fit work? | Qualification criteria, stage definitions, loss reasons |
| Referral system | Can a referrer identify whom to introduce and why now? | Introduction sources, language, tracking, follow-up |
| Intake and scoping | Are responsibilities, access, deliverables, and change control clear before work begins? | Intake forms, SOWs, kickoff records, scope changes |
| Delivery repeatability | Can the work be delivered consistently without all three founders improvising? | Stages, owners, templates, quality gates, handoffs |
| Measurement | Does Lucent know which activities create qualified pipeline and profitable delivery? | CRM, source attribution, margin, cycle time, conversion |

The lowest score is not automatically the binding constraint. Select the constraint whose repair most changes revenue or capacity, and explain the causal chain.

## Remaining engagement sequence

### Week 2: Decide the market and offer architecture

- Review the constraint map.
- Choose the priority buyer, urgent problem, and category position.
- Build three to five offers: entry diagnostic, core implementation, and continuation, plus at most two justified variants.
- Define outcome, scope, price, client responsibilities, exclusions, proof, and continuation threshold for each.
- Limit to one consolidated client revision.

### Week 3: Build the referral engine

- Define qualified introduction criteria.
- Segment referrers: client, board/funder, ecosystem, and professional peer.
- Provide ask language, timing triggers, a short forwardable description, follow-up cadence, and a minimum tracker design.
- Lucent populates the tracker and sends the asks.

### Week 4: Build intake-to-delivery operating system

- Map stages from qualified lead through handoff.
- Define owner, input, output, quality gate, and decision for each stage.
- Provide minimum templates: qualification intake, scoping brief, kickoff checklist, delivery cadence, change request, and handoff.
- Lucent configures tools and tests the process.

### Week 5: Validate and hand off

- Run one real or representative opportunity through the process.
- Correct only defects that prevent independent use.
- Finalize the diagnosis, offer set, referral playbook, and operating manual.
- Present a 90-day action plan with no more than five actions, named owners, dates, and measures.
- Identify any separately priced continuation. Do not begin it inside the current fee.

## Change-order triggers

Pause and rescope if Lucent asks Britt to:

- write or rebuild the website;
- configure CRM, automation, dashboards, or intake tooling;
- create campaigns, content calendars, or ongoing collateral;
- conduct broad market research or customer interviews not included in the evidence set;
- produce multiple positioning or offer directions for preference testing;
- staff or run sales and delivery operations;
- extend beyond one revision cycle per major deliverable;
- add stakeholders or business lines that materially change the diagnosis.

## Quality standard

The work is valuable because it forces decisions, connects evidence to the actual growth constraint, and leaves Lucent with a system its team can operate. It is not made more valuable by length, polish, or volume of artifacts.
