# Lucent kickoff and intake request

## Client-ready note

Hi Pearson, Ian, and Harrison,

To make the kickoff useful without creating a large preparation burden, please send or link the materials below by the end of the business day before our session. Existing materials are better than newly created ones. Please do not build anything for me.

1. The output from your Traction strategy session.
2. Your current service and pricing materials, including anything different from the public website.
3. Up to three recent proposals, ideally including one won, one lost, and one still open.
4. Up to three representative client examples, case studies, or completed-engagement summaries.
5. Any existing lead intake, scoping, kickoff, delivery, or handoff templates.
6. A simple list of active opportunities with source, buyer, offer, stage, approximate value, next step, and owner. An export is fine.
7. Any available 12-month revenue breakdown by client and type of work. Directional numbers are sufficient.

Please also come prepared to answer one question individually: If Lucent doubled its client volume in the next six months, what would break first?

We will use the kickoff to identify the actual constraint, align on the evidence we need, and decide where the diagnostic should go deepest. I will not ask you to complete a long questionnaire before we have established that focus.

Thanks,

Britt

## Internal intake rules

- Accept existing artifacts only. Do not ask Lucent to create polished summaries.
- Cap proposals and case examples at three each.
- Treat the public website as positioning evidence, not proof of buying behavior.
- Treat founder statements as hypotheses until supported by won work, pipeline, or delivery evidence.
- Store supplied confidential material only in the approved client workspace and DRIVE engagement context.
- Do not load personally identifiable client or health information into DRIVE. Request de-identified examples when needed.
